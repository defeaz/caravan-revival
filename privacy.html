Privacy

Caravan Revival collects the contact, vehicle, location and booking information you provide in order to arrange and deliver services, take payment, communicate about your appointment and keep appropriate business records.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Card details are not stored by Caravan Revival. Booking records are retained only as reasonably required for customer service, accounting and legal obligations and are not sold to third parties.

To ask what information is held about you, request a correction or raise a privacy question, use the website enquiry form or call 07794 527449.
